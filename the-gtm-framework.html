The GTM Framework

The GTM Framework is the system foundation behind all Caugia Consulting engagements. It brings clarity to complex revenue environments by structuring the entire GTM operation into one clear model — twelve segments across three layers.

Revenue Path

The Revenue Path includes every role directly responsible for creating, closing, and growing revenue. These six segments form the visible customer journey from first interaction to long-term expansion.

Marketing

Creates awareness and demand. Ensures the right companies enter your system with the right context.

Inbound SDR

Qualifies incoming interest with speed and clarity, converting demand into meaningful pipeline.

Outbound SDR

Generates new demand proactively by targeting strategic accounts when inbound slows.

Account Executive

Owns discovery, evaluation, value articulation, and deal orchestration — converting pipeline into revenue.

Customer Success

Ensures onboarding, adoption, and long-term value. Prevents churn through proactive engagement.

Account Management

Drives expansion, renewals, and long-term commercial health of accounts.

Support Layer

The Support Layer strengthens the Revenue Path. These roles provide expertise, technical depth, and precision to help customer-facing teams perform at their best.

Sales Engineers

Provide technical discovery, demonstrations, and solution architecture — critical for complex sales cycles.

GTM Engineers

Build and automate the GTM system backbone, managing routing, scoring, data flows, and operational automation.

Enablement Foundation

The Enablement Foundation ensures the entire GTM system operates with clarity, readiness, and consistency. These roles maintain alignment, improve execution, and protect scalability.

Sales Enablement

Builds onboarding, training, methodology, and readiness for all customer-facing teams.

RevOps

Owns forecasting, analytics, tooling, process design, and data governance.

Support

Ensures product reliability and customer problem resolution at scale.

GTM Leadership

Sets direction, alignment, and shared KPIs across the entire GTM system.
