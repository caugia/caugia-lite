The GTM Framework

The GTM Framework is the system foundation behind all Caugia Consulting engagements. It brings clarity to complex revenue environments by structuring the entire Go To Market operation into one model with twelve segments across three layers. It helps leadership see where information flows, where accountability breaks, and where energy is lost.

This framework is not a funnel. It is an operating system. It aligns teams, data, processes, and tools to create calm, predictable, and scalable growth.

Revenue Path

The first layer captures every team directly responsible for revenue generation. These segments represent the observable customer journey from first contact to expansion.

Marketing

Creates awareness and demand. Ensures that the right companies enter your revenue system with the right context.

Inbound SDR

Qualifies incoming interest with clarity and speed. Converts demand into meaningful pipeline.

Outbound SDR

Generates new demand proactively. Targets strategic accounts and fills the pipeline when inbound slows.

Account Executive

Owns discovery, value articulation, evaluation, and deal orchestration. Converts pipeline into revenue.

Customer Success

Ensures onboarding, adoption, and long term value. Prevents churn through proactive engagement.

Account Management

Drives expansion and renewals. Strengthens long term commercial relationships.

Support Layer

The support layer increases the effectiveness of the revenue path. These teams provide expertise, technical depth, and precision to help customer facing teams perform at their best.

Sales Engineers

Provide technical discovery, custom demos, and solution architecture. Critical for enterprise and complex sales cycles.

GTM Engineers

Build and automate the system backbone. Manage ICP, enrichment, routing, scoring, orchestration, and data flows across all revenue teams.

Enablement Foundation

This layer ensures that people, tools, and processes operate with clarity and consistency. It maintains alignment, creates readiness, and protects the scalability of the entire GTM system.

Sales Enablement

Builds onboarding, training, and methodology. Ensures every customer facing team has the skills to perform.

RevOps

Owns forecasting, analytics, process design, and tooling. Provides the single source of truth the system depends on.

Support

Resolves technical challenges and customer issues. Protects customer experience and product reliability.

GTM Leadership

Sets direction and shared KPIs. Aligns strategy, coordination, and decision making across the entire GTM system.

How the Framework Works

The GTM Framework reveals the hidden architecture behind predictable revenue. Instead of viewing GTM as a set of disconnected functions, it shows how segments interact, where handoffs break, and where systems need reinforcement. It gives leadership a common language for diagnosing issues and designing scalable solutions.

Most companies fail not because of a single weak team, but because segments operate without shared process, data, or rhythm. This framework exposes those gaps and creates a structured path to maturity.
