Services

Caugia Consulting helps executives and revenue leaders design calm, integrated GTM systems. We diagnose, we design, and we direct — always using The GTM Framework as the operating model behind predictable growth.

How we work

Diagnose

We start by observing your GTM system as it really operates: how information flows, where handoffs break, and where energy is lost between teams. The result is clarity — a true view of your GTM maturity, not assumptions.

Design

We create your GTM System Blueprint, a clear architecture connecting people, process, and tools. It defines how work moves, how accountability is shared, and how the system scales.

Direction

Design becomes action. We translate your blueprint into a Focus Plan — the smallest set of actions with the greatest impact. This creates rhythm and calm execution across all revenue teams.

Powered by The GTM Framework

All our work is grounded in The GTM Framework: twelve segments across three layers that reveal how revenue systems truly function. It provides leadership with one shared language for diagnosing problems, prioritizing investments, and scaling without friction.

GTM System Design
