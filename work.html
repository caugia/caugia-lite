Work

Every Caugia engagement follows a simple arc — discovery, design, and direction. Each phase delivers clarity, structure, and measurable progress toward one goal: a GTM system that runs predictably.

1. Discovery

We start by mapping your current GTM landscape. What systems, tools, and processes exist today? Where do leads, data, and accountability get lost? This phase includes:

  • Current-state architecture mapping
  • Tool inventory and usage audit
  • Gap analysis of team flows and data connections
  • Executive summary of pain points and opportunities

2. Design

Here the system takes shape. We build your GTM System Blueprint — a visual and operational model that defines how people, processes, and technology connect. You receive:

  • Custom architecture (as-is and to-be)
  • Tools matrix with consolidation and AI recommendations
  • Team structure and collaboration model
  • Prioritized roadmap with phases and dependencies

3. Direction

Finally, we turn design into action. We create a one-year Focus Plan with clear priorities, metrics, and milestones. This includes:

  • ROI and productivity projections
  • Implementation milestones by quarter
  • Leadership alignment workshop
  • Ongoing advisory or partner support if required

Deliverables at a Glance

GTM System Blueprint — the foundation of your predictable revenue engine.
Tools Matrix — a clear view of your tech ecosystem.
Focus Plan — one year of direction and measurable progress.
Alignment Session — executive clarity and commitment.

Engagement Models

Every engagement is tailored, but most fall into three formats:

  • Assessment (1–2 weeks) — Rapid diagnostic and recommendations (€5K–15K)
  • System Design (4–6 weeks) — Full architecture, playbooks, and roadmap (€25K–50K)
  • Implementation Partner (3–6 months) — Hands-on delivery and training (€10K–25K/month)

Each project is visual, data-driven, and tool-agnostic. We don’t sell software — we build the system that makes software work for you.
