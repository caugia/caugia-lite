About

Most go-to-market organizations struggle not because of effort but because of fragmentation. Too many tools, disconnected teams, and reactive processes make growth unpredictable. Caugia Studio helps companies bring structure and focus back into their revenue systems.

Founded by Tom Meijer, who built and scaled global revenue operations at Contentsquare and Greenly, Caugia Studio applies system design principles to the way companies grow. We design GTM architectures that align strategy, people, and execution into a single coherent framework.

Our philosophy is simple: People, Process, and Performance must work in balance. When these three pillars align, revenue becomes predictable and growth compounds naturally.
