About Caugia Consulting

Caugia Consulting helps executives and GTM leaders turn commercial complexity into predictable revenue systems. We design architectures that align data, teams, and growth so that performance becomes measurable and calm. The name Caugia comes from Coye la Forêt, once called Caugia in 799. It stands for clarity before movement — the same principle that guides our work and underpins The GTM Framework.

Founder

Tom Meijer
Founder and Revenue Systems Architect

Tom is originally from the Netherlands and has spent more than fifteen years building and leading GTM organizations in France. He began his career in sales, later led commercial teams, and helped scale companies from early traction to global operations. At Contentsquare and Greenly he worked where revenue and technology meet, guiding teams as they evolved from reactive selling to structured, data driven performance. Through Caugia Consulting he now helps others design systems that scale with intent and operate with discipline.
