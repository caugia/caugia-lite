About Caugia Studio

Caugia Studio helps executives and GTM leaders turn commercial chaos into predictable revenue systems. We design architectures that align data, teams, and growth so performance becomes measurable and calm.

The name Caugia has deep roots. It originates from Coye-la-Forêt, a village near Paris once called Caugia in 799. The name symbolizes continuity and evolution, ideas central to how we design systems that endure. It also reflects our belief that clarity begins with calm.

Founder

Tom Meijer
Founder and Revenue Systems Architect

Originally from the Netherlands, Tom has spent more than fifteen years building and leading GTM organizations in France. He began his career in sales, later led commercial teams, and helped scale companies from early traction to global operations. At Contentsquare and Greenly, he worked where revenue and technology meet, guiding teams as they evolved from reactive selling to structured, data-driven performance.

He grew up professionally in organizations that built products powered by machine learning and AI. There he learned how technology can amplify human judgment when designed with clarity. Over his career, Tom has led teams from a handful of people to more than a hundred across Europe, the United States, and Asia, reporting directly to executive leadership. Through Caugia Studio, he now helps others design systems that scale with intent and operate with discipline.

Origin

The name Caugia comes from the Latin word Caugiacum, the earliest name for Coye-la-Forêt, where the studio was founded. More than twelve centuries ago, it described a clearing at the edge of the forest, a place where travelers paused before continuing their journey. That idea still defines us today: progress begins with clarity before movement.
