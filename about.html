About Caugia Consulting

Caugia Consulting helps executives and GTM leaders transform complexity into coherent growth systems. We design commercial architectures that align data, teams, and decisions, bringing measurable rhythm to performance and calm to leadership. Every engagement is built on The GTM Framework, a structured yet flexible method that brings consistency to how organizations grow.

The name Caugia comes from Coye la Forêt, a village near Paris once known as Caugia in 799. Historically, it described a clearing at the edge of the forest, a place to pause before moving forward. That meaning shapes our philosophy: clarity before movement. Every system we design begins with observation, not reaction, so that progress becomes deliberate and lasting.

Founder

Tom Meijer
Founder and Revenue Systems Architect

Tom has spent more than fifteen years building and leading GTM organizations across Europe. His career spans the full GTM spectrum, from sales and operations to data, enablement, and leadership. At Contentsquare and Greenly, he worked where revenue, product, and technology converge, helping teams evolve from activity-driven growth to structured, predictable systems.

He founded Caugia Consulting to help organizations rediscover coherence in how they grow, balancing structure with judgment and data with direction. His work combines operational precision with a calm, system-level view of performance, guiding leadership teams toward clarity that endures.
