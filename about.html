About Caugia Consulting

Caugia Consulting helps executives and GTM leaders turn complexity into coherent growth systems. The GTM Framework is the method we use to align data, structure, and judgment so that direction lasts and performance becomes predictable.

The name Caugia comes from Coye la Forêt, a village near Paris that was recorded as Caugia in the year 799. The word described a clearing at the edge of the forest. A place to pause before moving forward. That idea guides our work. Clarity before movement. Calm observation before design. Design before scale.

Founder

Tom Meijer
Founder and Revenue Systems Architect

Tom has built and led GTM organizations across Europe. He worked where revenue, product, and data meet. At scale ups and global teams he saw the same pattern. Growth breaks coherence when work is not guided by a shared model. He founded Caugia Consulting to bring that model to leaders who want structure without noise and progress without rush.
