About Caugia Consulting

Caugia Consulting helps executives and GTM leaders turn commercial complexity into predictable revenue systems. We design architectures that align data, teams, and growth so that performance becomes measurable and calm.

The name Caugia has deep roots. It originates from Coye la Forêt, a village near Paris once called Caugia in 799. The name symbolizes continuity and evolution, ideas central to how we design systems that endure. It also reflects our belief that clarity begins with calm.

Founder

Tom Meijer
Founder and Revenue Systems Architect

Tom is originally from the Netherlands and has spent more than fifteen years building and leading GTM organizations in France. He began his career in sales, later led commercial teams, and helped scale companies from early traction to global operations. At Contentsquare and Greenly he worked where revenue and technology meet, guiding teams as they evolved from reactive selling to structured, data driven performance.

Over his career Tom has led teams from a handful of people to more than a hundred across Europe, the United States, and Asia. Through Caugia Consulting he now helps others design systems that scale with intent and operate with discipline.

Origin

The name Caugia comes from the Latin word Caugiacum, the earliest name for Coye la Forêt where the consulting practice was founded. More than twelve centuries ago it described a clearing at the edge of the forest, a place where travelers paused before continuing their journey. That idea still defines us today. Progress begins with clarity before movement.
