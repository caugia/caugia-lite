About Caugia Consulting

Caugia Consulting helps organizations bring clarity, structure and predictability into their Go-to-Market system. We work with leaders who want calm, focus and a mature approach to growth.

Why Caugia exists

Modern Go-to-Market teams operate in an environment that becomes more complex each year. Tools increase, workflows spread across disconnected systems and teams lose the shared rhythm needed for stable performance.

Pipeline becomes inconsistent. Ownership becomes unclear. Decisions are made too late. Results depend too much on individual effort and not enough on a well designed system.

Caugia was created to improve this. We help leadership redesign their Go-to-Market system so it becomes clear, structured and easy to understand. When the system is calm and logical, performance becomes predictable. Teams understand how work moves, what matters and why.

Our philosophy

Organizations rarely struggle because of one weak function. They struggle because the space between functions is not aligned. Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, Product Marketing and Revenue Operations often work with different priorities and definitions.

We focus on the system between teams. We make roles, handoffs and rhythms explicit so that everyone understands how value is created and where it is blocked. Growth does not come from intensity. It comes from structure that teams can trust every week.

How we are different

  • System first, not channels. We look at the complete Go-to-Market system, not only at one team or one tool.
  • Deterministic logic before AI. We start with clear rules, scoring and structure. AI is used as a controlled layer for synthesis, not as a black box.
  • Calm and direct. We avoid hype and noise. You get clear language, precise diagnostics and practical guidance.
  • Built for recurring revenue models. Everything is designed for B2B companies with recurring revenue who need reliable and predictable performance across the full customer lifecycle.

What we bring

  • A proprietary diagnostic system that measures Go-to-Market maturity across twelve pillars.
  • Clear frameworks that replace guesswork with a structured path toward a stronger GTM engine.
  • The ability to identify your highest leverage fixes early, so effort is not spread across low impact work.
  • A calm, structured way of working that reduces chaos instead of adding new layers of complexity.
  • Alignment across leadership through a shared operating language for the full revenue system.

Who is behind Caugia

Caugia Consulting was founded by Tom Meijer. Tom spent nearly a decade building Go-to-Market systems at high growth SaaS companies, including Contentsquare and Greenly. He saw the same patterns repeat across organizations: complex tool stacks, unclear ownership and growth that depended on a few strong individuals.

Caugia was created as a response to this. The GTM Framework and the GTM Intelligence Report give leadership teams a structured way to understand their current system, decide what to improve first and execute change with confidence.
