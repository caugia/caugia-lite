About Caugia Studio

Founded by Tom Meijer, Caugia Studio helps GTM and revenue leaders turn complexity into clarity. After years leading global growth and revenue teams, Tom saw that most organizations don’t lack talent—they lack systems that align strategy, data, and execution.

Caugia was born from that insight: a studio that designs integrated revenue architectures. Each project blends analytical rigor with creative systems thinking—bridging the gap between data and direction.
