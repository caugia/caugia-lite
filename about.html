About Caugia Studio

Caugia Studio helps executives and GTM leaders turn commercial chaos into predictable revenue engines. We design integrated systems that align data, teams, and growth so that performance becomes measurable and repeatable.

The name Caugia has deep roots. It originates from Coye-la-Forêt, a village near Paris known in 799 as Caugia. The spirit of the name reflects continuity and evolution, two ideas central to how we build systems today.

The idea behind Caugia Studio is simple. Most organizations do not lack ambition or technology; they lack coherence. As they grow, their go-to-market systems become fragmented. Teams chase different metrics, data lives in silos, and leadership loses visibility into what truly drives performance.

After fifteen years building and leading global revenue organizations at Contentsquare and Greenly, I saw the same challenge everywhere. Brilliant people, sophisticated tools, but no shared operating model. Growth felt reactive, not designed.

The rise of AI has only amplified this complexity. Automation moves faster than alignment, and every new tool adds another layer of noise. At the same time, economic pressure forces companies to search for efficiency, to consolidate, and to achieve more with less.

Caugia was created to bring order back into that reality. We help leadership teams design GTM systems that balance growth and efficiency, where every process, tool, and role supports one connected engine. The goal is not to do more, but to make every part of the system work together.

Once leaders can see the entire system and understand how people, processes, and performance interact, decisions become clear and growth becomes predictable.

Caugia brings calm intelligence to complex organizations and transforms fragmented operations into measurable performance.

Founder

Tom Meijer
Founder and Revenue Systems Architect

Tom is originally from the Netherlands and has spent more than fifteen years building and leading global GTM organizations in France. He began his career in sales, later led commercial teams, and helped scale companies from early traction to international growth. At Contentsquare and Greenly, he worked at the intersection of revenue and technology, guiding teams as they evolved from reactive selling to structured, data-driven performance.

Growing up in organizations that built products powered by machine learning and AI, Tom learned how technology can amplify human judgment when it is designed with clarity. He has led teams from two people to over a hundred, expanded operations from a single market to global coverage, and reported directly to executive leadership throughout his career. Through Caugia Studio, he now helps other leaders design GTM systems that scale with intent and operate with discipline.

Origin

The name Caugia comes from the original Latin word Caugiacum, the earliest name for Coye-la-Forêt, the French village where the studio was founded. More than twelve centuries ago, it described a clearing on the edge of the forest, a place where people paused before continuing their path. That idea still defines us today: progress begins with clarity before movement.
