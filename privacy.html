Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2025

This privacy notice explains how data collected through The GTM Score assessment and this website is handled during Caugia’s pre-launch phase. Caugia Consulting is not yet a registered business entity. A full GDPR-compliant privacy policy will be published upon official company registration in 2026.

What we collect

When you complete The GTM Score assessment, we may collect:

  • Your name (optional)
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Your company name (optional)
  • Your responses to the assessment questions

How this information is used

During the pre-launch period, your information is used only to:

  • Display your GTM Score result
  • Improve the GTM Score assessment and The GTM Framework
  • Understand how leaders use the assessment

Your data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

Where your data is processed

To operate the GTM Score assessment and this website, the following service providers may process your assessment responses or technical data:

  • Typeform (assessment platform)
  • Vercel (site hosting)
  • Cloudflare (security, CDN, domain management)
  • OpenAI (AI-assisted content creation and internal analysis)
  • Anthropic (AI-assisted content creation and internal analysis)
  • Mistral (AI-assisted content creation and internal analysis)
  • Make (internal workflow automation)
  • Brevo (email infrastructure — not yet active)

These platforms do not use your data for advertising and do not have permission to sell or share it.

Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your assessment data at any time. To do so, contact:

tom@caugia.com

Future updates

When Caugia Consulting becomes a registered business, this notice will be replaced by a full GDPR-compliant privacy policy including:

  • Legal entity details
  • Data retention periods
  • Cookie policy and consent mechanism
  • Lead capture and analytics usage
  • Details of all commercial processing activities
