The GTM Score

The GTM Score is a concise, structured assessment that measures the maturity of your Go To Market system. Eleven focused questions reveal strengths, structural gaps, and the direction needed to build a predictable revenue engine.

What you'll discover

  • ✓ Your GTM maturity score on a 0–100 scale

How the GTM Score works

The GTM Score evaluates the core elements of your go-to-market system across eleven focused questions. Each response contributes to a raw score that is translated into a clear 0–100 maturity scale.

The score reflects how aligned your strategy, execution, data, and processes are — and how predictable your revenue system can become as a result.

Frequently asked questions

Does the assessment require my email?

No. Your results appear instantly on screen. You only share your email if you decide to continue working with Caugia later.

How accurate is the score?

The score is directionally accurate. It is clear enough to indicate your maturity level and precise enough to highlight your most important priorities.

How long does it take?

Around five minutes. The questions are simple and based on the core elements of a modern GTM system.
