How the GTM Score works

The GTM Score evaluates the core elements of your go-to-market system across 11 focused questions. Each response contributes to a raw score between 0 and 55, which is smoothly translated into a 0–100 maturity level for clarity and comparability.

The score reflects how aligned your strategy, execution, data, and processes are — and how predictable your revenue system can become as a result.

Frequently asked questions

Does the assessment require my email?

No. Your results appear instantly on screen. You only share your email if you want to continue working with Caugia later.

How accurate is the score?

The score is directionally accurate: clear enough to indicate your maturity level and precise enough to highlight your top priorities.

How long does it take?

Around 5 minutes. The questions are simple and based on the core elements of any modern GTM system.
