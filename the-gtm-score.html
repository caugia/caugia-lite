The GTM Score

The GTM Score is a structured assessment of how mature your Go To Market system really is. It measures your organization across the twelve segments of The GTM Framework and translates complexity into one clear view of where you stand.

You can use the score to align leadership, to prioritise investment in GTM, or to prepare for a deeper system design project. It is a starting point for honest conversations about how your organization grows.

What you can expect

  • A clear view of your strengths and gaps across the GTM system.
  • A maturity indication per segment, not just a single number.
  • Language you can use with leadership and teams to discuss priorities.

Start the GTM Score

The assessment takes around fifteen minutes. Answer as honestly as possible for the most useful result.
