Your GTM Score

Result: Unstable (41–50)

Your GTM Score is 0/100.

You have some foundations in place, but the system is fragile. When key people leave, when a few big deals slip, or when demand slows, everything feels exposed.

The organization often works in reaction mode. Knowledge lives in a few people, not in a shared system, which makes it hard to scale and hard to forecast with confidence.

What this means

Companies at this stage often see large swings in win rates and forecast accuracy. Results depend heavily on individual effort and memory rather than on clear, documented and shared ways of working.

The good news is that your system has the beginnings of structure. With focused work on process, documentation, and simple governance, you can move to stability in a predictable way.

Where to focus first

  • Document your core sales process from first contact to closed won or lost.
  • Create shared definitions for stages, handoffs, and qualification across marketing, SDR, and sales.
  • Start a simple win and loss review so the team learns systematically rather than anecdotally.
  • Introduce a basic, data informed forecast method instead of relying only on intuition.

Moving from unstable to stable

The next stage is about building confidence in how your GTM system behaves. A deeper assessment can map exactly where instability comes from and how to redesign the system so that performance becomes more predictable.
