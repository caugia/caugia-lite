What this means

Organizations in the Unstable stage typically experience:

  • • 20–30% forecast variance
  • • inconsistent win rates with quarter-to-quarter swings of 10–15 points
  • • increasing dependency on individual heroics instead of system-driven consistency
  • • tribal knowledge instead of documented process
  • • limited clarity on why deals are won or lost
  • • leadership constantly in reactive mode

Your top priorities

  • Document your sales process. Move from intuition and individual talent to a repeatable, measurable workflow.
  • Fix competitive intelligence. Create structured win/loss analysis. Understand root causes — not anecdotes.
  • Improve forecasting accuracy. Shift from “gut feel” to clear stage criteria, exit rules, and data validation.
