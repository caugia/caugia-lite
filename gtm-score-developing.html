What this means

Companies at the Developing stage typically experience:

  • • 15–20% longer sales cycles than optimized peers
  • • friction between sales and marketing over lead quality
  • • pipeline data that is incomplete or unreliable
  • • loss of 10–15% of winnable deals due to process gaps
  • • inconsistent competitive messaging

Your top priorities

  • Improve pipeline visibility. Introduce real-time dashboards and enforce data quality so leadership can see issues early.
  • Align sales and marketing on lead quality. Create clear MQL criteria, automate handoffs, and build shared accountability.
  • Strengthen competitive intelligence. Build structured battlecards and train teams on value and differentiation.
