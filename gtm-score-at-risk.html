Your GTM Score: At Risk

Your Go To Market system is structurally exposed. Critical gaps are directly affecting revenue performance, making forecasting, execution, and growth difficult to control.

Your score

0/100

What this means

Companies in the At Risk stage typically face:

  • • 30–40% forecast variance
  • • 50%+ loss rates in competitive deals
  • • unclear ownership between marketing, sales, and success
  • • long and unpredictable sales cycles
  • • significant time wasted on bad-fit leads
  • • CAC payback periods exceeding 18 months

Your top priorities

  • Define your ICP and target quality. Stop spending resources on companies that will not convert or renew.
  • Establish basic sales & marketing alignment. Create shared MQL criteria, a clear handoff process, and a common qualification model.
  • Improve pipeline visibility. You cannot fix what you cannot see. Create a reliable view of stage progression and conversion.
