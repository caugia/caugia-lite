Your GTM Score

Result: At Risk (0–40)

Your GTM Score is 0/100.

Your go to market system has structural gaps that put revenue at risk. Forecasts are likely unpredictable, handoffs feel fragile, and teams spend too much energy fighting symptoms instead of fixing causes.

At this stage, growth depends heavily on individual heroics. When a key person leaves or a big deal slips, the entire plan feels uncertain.

What this means

Companies at this maturity level often experience large swings in forecast accuracy, frequent surprise losses, long sales cycles, and difficulty understanding which motions really create revenue.

The opportunity is significant. With the right structure, you can move from constant firefighting to clear, calm direction within a few quarters.

Where to focus first

  • Clarify your ideal customer profile so teams stop spending time on poor fit opportunities.
  • Define a simple shared view of the funnel so marketing, SDR, and sales speak the same language.
  • Create basic pipeline visibility so leadership can see risk early and act before the quarter is over.
  • Document one clear sales process so results depend less on individuals and more on system design.

Turning At Risk into progress

The next step is to move from a single score to a clear diagnosis. The GTM Score shows you that the system is at risk. A deeper assessment shows exactly where the structure breaks and which few changes will restore stability.
