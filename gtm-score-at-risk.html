Your GTM Score: At Risk

Your Go To Market system is structurally exposed. Critical gaps are directly affecting revenue performance, which makes forecasting, execution, and growth difficult to control.

Your score

0/100

What this means

Companies in the At Risk stage typically face:

  • • 30–40% forecast variance
  • • more than half of competitive deals lost
  • • unclear ownership between marketing, sales, and customer success
  • • long and unpredictable sales cycles
  • • significant time spent on poor fit opportunities
  • • customer acquisition costs that are difficult to recover

Your top priorities

  • Clarify your ideal customer profile.
    Narrow your focus to companies that can actually convert, implement, and renew.
  • Establish basic sales and marketing alignment.
    Agree on qualification criteria, define clear handoffs, and create a shared view of what a good opportunity looks like.
  • Increase pipeline visibility.
    Build a reliable picture of each stage so that leadership can see where value is leaking.
